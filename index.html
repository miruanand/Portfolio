FEATURED HACKATHON

SIH 2025 — SELECTED

AgroBot

ML-Powered Precision Agriculture Robot — Smart India Hackathon 2025

Our team built a field robot that finds a problem before it spreads. A Raspberry Pi 5 running a computer-vision classification pipeline scans crops for disease in real time, then triggers a targeted spray mechanism — cutting chemical wastage instead of blanket-spraying an entire field. A companion mobile app gives live monitoring, remote control, and field analytics, so the robot's findings are actually usable back at the farmhouse.

The project was selected for Smart India Hackathon 2025, and the detection-and-spraying mechanism went on to become a published patent application — one of the more concrete outcomes of a 36-hour hackathon I've had.
